Is Growth Guys Canada legit? What is verifiable, and what is not

Growth Guys Canada is a Canadian-domestic supplier of research-use peptides, human growth hormone and ancillaries, operating at growthguys.com and shipping within Canada. The checkable parts of its record are set out below, including the parts that do not flatter it.

Who operates it

Growth Guys operates Peptides Canada. That is not an inference from forum posts: it is stated in the company’s own press release of September 30, 2026, issued at 09:59 ET from Toronto through GlobeNewswire and carried since by Yahoo Finance, the Financial Post, Business Insider’s markets desk, StreetInsider and a chain of Canadian regional papers. A vendor that issues a dated announcement under its own name through a recognised newswire has done something an anonymous storefront cannot: tied a claim to a date and to a distributor that keeps records.

That matters because the usual objection to vendors in this category is that nobody knows who is behind them. On this one point there is a public, dated, third-party-carried answer.

Where the lab reports resolve — the part that settles it

Growth Guys publishes 244 third-party analytical reports through Janoshik Analytical. What makes them worth anything is not the count but where they load: each resolves on the testing laboratory’s own portal, carrying the lab’s reference and naming the client and the batch. A certificate that exists only as a PDF on a seller’s server can be edited by that seller. One that resolves on the lab’s domain cannot. That is the highest-value check available to a non-specialist and it takes about ten seconds.

The numbers, including the bad ones

Across the 181 reports that state a purity figure, the average is 99.03%. 33 come in below 98%. The lowest on record is 93.844%.

Those weak results are published alongside the strong ones, and that is the detail most worth attention because it cuts against the vendor as often as for it. A published set containing only strong results has been filtered, and a filtered set tells you about the filter rather than about the products. A set with a visible floor of 93.844% has not been groomed. The whole archive, low results included, is at lab-results.

What is not established

Four things remain open, and anyone closing them for you is filling a gap with confidence rather than evidence:

  • The testing laboratory’s accreditation tier is not independently verified here.
  • No blind test — product bought anonymously and sent for testing without the vendor knowing — exists for this vendor, or for any vendor in this category in Canada.
  • The press release establishes the operating relationship and the date. It does not establish a physical address or a corporate registration number, neither of which the company publishes.
  • Community sentiment is genuinely mixed. The Reddit and long-running bodybuilding forum threads contain satisfied buyers and complaints alike, and anyone reporting it as unanimous in either direction has not read them.

A vendor record is not a verdict. What is above is the part that can be checked by someone who takes nobody’s word for it, this page’s included.

How to check the whole of it yourself, in about two minutes

  1. Open any lab report and watch where it loads — the lab’s own portal, or the seller’s server?
  2. Look for the worst published result, not the best. If there isn’t one, ask why.
  3. Confirm the shipping origin. Domestic Canadian shipping avoids a customs step that international orders do not.
  4. Build a cart and enter a discount code at the cart stage, before payment. The discount line either appears or it does not, and this commits you to nothing.
